# Research findings, 2026-08-24

Everything from a parallel research effort into what the bot is not doing at maximum. Written to
disk because it was gathered into a conversation and would otherwise be lost. Findings are recorded
with their evidence and their provenance, and vendor claims are marked as such — several of the most
widely repeated numbers in this space turned out to be marketing.

Nothing here has been acted on except where a commit is named.

## 1. Infrastructure: almost nothing expensive is worth buying

The single most useful result. Our entry path was timed call by call (MT194) and the assumption
about where the time goes was wrong.

```
getTokenLargestAccounts   571 ms   49% of the path
getLatestBlockhash        204 ms
getAccountInfo (curve)    181 ms
jupiter /swap/v1/quote     93 ms
jupiter /swap/v1/swap      72 ms
getTokenAccountsByOwner    42 ms
TOTAL                    1163 ms
```

**The two Jupiter round trips are 14% of the path.** Building the pump.fun instruction offline —
which the pinned `@pump-fun/pump-sdk@1.36.0` already supports, see §5 — would replace 165 ms of
Jupiter with a `getMultipleAccounts` of comparable cost. Net saving approximately zero. Not pursued.

### What the independent data says about paid options

| Option | Verdict | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Jito global block engine** | **Actively worse.** p50 633 ms / +2 slots vs **147 ms / +1 slot for the free public RPC**, consistent across five runs. MEV-protected sends enter a bundle auction that holds them. | free + $105–780/mo in tips |
| Staked connections / SWQoS | Congestion insurance, ~0 ms normally. Agave 3.1 quadrupled unstaked connection slots 500→2,000, handing small senders most of the benefit free. | $49–1,000/mo |
| Geyser gRPC | **No independent benchmark exists.** Best defensible figure is a vendor conceding p90 374 ms websocket → 215 ms gRPC. PublicNode serves it **free** — test before buying. | $0–499/mo |
| DoubleZero Edge / dedicated nodes | ~20 ms p50 for more than our likely gross. | $1,500–2,900/mo |
| **Vultr High Frequency, Amsterdam or Frankfurt** | **Worth it** — but for jitter, not the mean. Measured jitter 18→103 ms vs regional differences of only 7 ms. | **$6–24/mo** |

Leader geography (Glassnode independent probe network): Frankfurt 25.9% of slots, Amsterdam 21.0%,
London 12.4% — **Europe is 67–68% of all blocks**, North America 20.4%, Asia 10.5%.

**Total defensible spend: under $30/month.** Nothing above $200/mo is justifiable at this scale, and
no evidence was found that anything above $500/mo helps at all.

### Two dates that break things

- **Jito ShredStream shuts down 2026-09-05.** Anything touching it stops working.
- **Slot time cut 400 ms → 350 ms on 2026-08-22** (SIMD-0525), the first reduction since genesis,
  with 300/250/200 ms on the roadmap. Historical slot→seconds conversions in our corpus remain
  correct; any LIVE trades-per-second figure is 14% understated.

### Priority fees: already above market, not a lever

Our own transactions decoded from their bytes: **646,391 and 618,364 microLamports per compute
unit**, against a curve-local market of p50 273,972 and p75/p95 409,608. Jupiter's default is
generous. `getRecentPrioritizationFees` **on the curve account** is the correct query (fees are
local to writable accounts), and priority genuinely matters more on a bonding curve than elsewhere —
every buy write-locks the same account, so the scheduler orders conflicting transactions by priority
and order-within-slot *is* fill price. But we are not underpaying.

## 2. The stage-shift thesis — the biggest strategic finding, NOT YET VERIFIED

Two independent datasets agree the fee toll falls roughly 4x from the bonding curve to graduated
PumpSwap tokens, and that the volume is inverted:

| Venue | 30d fees | 30d volume | Effective bps | Share of Solana DEX volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pump.fun bonding curve | $39.91M | $2.298B | **~174** | 4.2% |
| PumpSwap | $69.34M | $17.89B | **~39** | 32.9% |

Source: DefiLlama API pulled 2026-08-24, whose PumpSwap adapter already filters wash pools at
TVL ≥ $5k and ≥50 unique traders.

**We trade the most expensive, most contested 4% of Solana DEX volume.**

Supporting evidence that the crowd is on the curve and not the pool: a peer-reviewed bot taxonomy
(arXiv:2607.28424, ASE 2026 — 586 repos linked to 200 on-chain addresses across 44.1M transactions)
fingerprints trading-operation bots as **80.9% confined to pump.fun**, with MEV clusters at 95%+ on
Raydium and Meteora DLMM. **PumpSwap and Orca are absent from the venue fingerprints.**

### The structural vacuum

Pine Analytics (independent, full-chain, 1 Apr – 14 Jul 2026) measured proprietary-AMM share of
volume by asset class. Prop AMMs are 28.0% of all Solana DEX volume:

| Asset class | Prop AMM share |
|---|---|
| Wrapped ETH vs stables | 96% |
| SOL/dollars | ~61% |
| CEX-listed large caps | 38–68% |
| Perp-listed memecoins | 17–44% |
| **Assets priced only on-chain** | **0%** |

The mechanism is **hedgeability**, not oversight: a professional market maker will not quote what it
cannot hedge against an external reference. This is the structural analogue of our own slot-zero
finding at the opposite end of a coin's life — **and unlike a latency race, it does not close when
someone notices it**, because the constraint is a risk policy.

### Our 80% collapse rate is the base rate, not a filter failure

- CoinGecko, 18.67M tokens, Jan 2024 – Jun 2026: **4.55% still traded past 90 days; 68.67% recorded
  their last trade on the day they launched.**
- arXiv:2602.14860, 655,770 tokens: **92.22% of tokens with ≥30 swaps had a detected dump event**
  (4-sigma control chart). Creator incentives measured: selling strictly *before* graduation beats
  selling immediately after.

This is an argument for changing the population, not the filter.

**STATUS: unverified against our own tape.** The fee ladder is in our trade cache (fields 10/11/12 =
lp/protocol/creator bps), so this is cheap to check and must be checked before any strategy change.

## 3. What is closed, with mechanisms

**Sandwiching — arithmetic, not judgement.** Measured mid-2026 sandwich profit is ~$0.9M/month
across **203 attackers** → median attacker ~$4,500/month gross, power-law distributed so the median
is worse than the mean suggests. Table-stakes infrastructure is $3,200–5,200/mo single-region. **The
median sandwicher does not clear the infrastructure bill.** Network economics are contracting
alongside: Solana REV Q2 2026 $51.0M (−43% QoQ), priority fees −45%, Jito tips −50%.

**Copy-trading — now with the mechanism.** WWW'26 (arXiv:2601.08641, NTU + UCL): on a bonding curve
price impact depends on *ordering*, so **a zero-latency copier still fills worse than the leader**.
The imitation penalty is mechanical, not a latency artifact. This independently predicts our own
MT178/MT179 result — that a physically impossible zero-lag mirror still loses — so that family is
closed and should not be re-tested.

**"Trade the ignored launchpad" is dead.** Live 7-day launchpad fees: **pump.fun 95.06%** of $11.97M.
LetsBonk fell from ~66% of daily launches and 209 graduations/day (Jul 2025) to ~1.7% and 5/day.
Believe did **$79 in 7 days**. Those venues are un-botted because they are un-traded.

## 4. Regime timing: measured, and there is nothing to time

Daily dispersion of the strategy's own growth across 8 days of tape (MT197):

```
mean 0.0256   sd 0.0081   worst 0.0141   best 0.0354
days negative: 0 of 8
```

Nothing predicts next-day growth. Spearman against the following day: graduation rate −0.143,
launches −0.179, volume −0.179, own prior growth −0.679 — on 7 day pairs, which is noise. **Standing
down on bad days would gain nothing**, which closes a lever that looked like it might be worth 20x.

## 5. pump.fun instruction format (2026), verified against live mainnet

Kept because it is hard to reconstruct and every public guide is incomplete. Verified against the
official IDL *and* ~200 decoded live transactions at slot 441,402,656.

**`buy`** — discriminator `66063d1201daebea` = `sha256("global:buy")[0..8]`.
Args: `amount: u64`, `max_sol_cost: u64`, `track_volume: OptionBool` (variable length; **24-byte data
with no trailing byte is the dominant live form and works**).

**`sell`** — discriminator `33e685a4017f83ad`. Args `amount: u64`, `min_sol_output: u64`, always
exactly 24 bytes.

**THE IDL IS INCOMPLETE AND THIS IS THE part that fails silently.** The deployed program requires
remaining accounts present in no IDL and in no public guide:

- **`bonding_curve_v2`** — PDA `["bonding-curve-v2", mint]`
- **`buyback_fee_recipient`** — one of 8 buyback recipients

`Global.buyback_basis_points` is **5000** on-chain, so half the protocol fee routes to the buyback
recipient. 100% of decoded live buys and sells pass both. Omitting them gives error `6027
NotEnoughRemainingAccounts`. Solana Tracker's otherwise-correct guide is 2 and 3 accounts short.

Account counts: legacy `buy` **18** (16 IDL + 2), legacy `sell` **17** (14 IDL + 3). Note the
asymmetry: in `sell`, `creator_vault` is at index 8 *before* `token_program`; in `buy` it is at 9
*after*. Confirmed in both the IDL and live transactions.

**PDA seeds — the hyphen/underscore split is not uniform:**

| Account | Seeds |
|---|---|
| `global` | `["global"]` → `4wTV1YmiEkRvAtNtsSGPtUrqRYQMe5SKy2uB4Jjaxnjf` |
| `bonding_curve` | `["bonding-curve", mint]` (hyphen) |
| `bonding_curve_v2` | `["bonding-curve-v2", mint]` |
| `creator_vault` | `["creator-vault", creator]` (hyphen) |
| `global_volume_accumulator` | `["global_volume_accumulator"]` (underscores) → `Hq2wp8uJ9jCPsYgNHex8RtqdvMPfVGoYwjvF1ATiwn2Y` |
| `user_volume_accumulator` | `["user_volume_accumulator", user]` (underscores) |
| `event_authority` | `["__event_authority"]` → `Ce6TQqeHC9p8KetsN6JsjHK7UTZk7nasjjnr7XxXp9F1` |
| `fee_config` | `["fee_config", <32 bytes of pump program id>]` **under `pfeeUxB…`** → `8Wf5TiAheLUqBrKXeYg2JtAFFMWtKdG2BSFgqUcPVwTt` |

**The fee program is both passed as an account and invoked by CPI** — `invoke [2]` at ~3,302 CU on
every buy/sell, returning `(lp, protocol, creator)` bps for the coin's market-cap tier.

`BondingCurve` layout after the 8-byte discriminator `17b7f83760d8ac60`: `virtual_token_reserves u64,
virtual_quote_reserves u64, real_token_reserves u64, real_quote_reserves u64, token_total_supply u64,
complete bool, creator pubkey, is_mayhem_mode bool, is_cashback_coin bool, quote_mint pubkey`.
**`creator` is at offset 49.**

**`buy_exact_sol_in`** (disc `38fc74089edfcd5f`) takes a SOL budget rather than an exact token count
and shares the identical 18-account layout — almost certainly the right instruction for a bot, since
it removes the need to invert the curve ourselves.

Legacy `buy`/`sell` **cannot trade non-SOL-quoted coins**; `Global.whitelisted_quote_mints` now
includes USDC. Gate on `BondingCurve.quote_mint == Pubkey::default()`.

### Offline building is feasible and was deliberately not done

`@pump-fun/pump-sdk@1.36.0` is installed, version-pinned, and has **zero call sites**. It exposes
offline `buyInstructions`/`sellInstructions`. The repo already owns `compileMessage`, `encodeMessage`
(v0 only), `encodeUnsignedTransaction`, and `writeSignature`. Blockers are ours, not the builder's:
`DEFAULT_ALLOWED_PROGRAMS` does not include the pump program, and `verifyEffect` requires a
simulation round trip that cannot be removed. **Not pursued because the timing says it saves nothing
(§1).**
